# Image Processing Course Labs
 
Lab assignments for the **Image Processing** course taught by **Mme Wided Souid** at **INSAT (Institut National des Sciences Appliquées et de Technologie)**, Tunis.
 

---
 
## Labs Overview
 
| Lab | Title | Topics |
|-----|-------|--------|
| [TP2](Histogram_Enhancement) | **Histogram Manipulation & Medical Image Enhancement** | Histogram equalization, CLAHE, AHE, HE/CHE/QDHE comparison, MSE/PSNR/SD metrics |
| [TP4](Image_Segmentation) | **Image Segmentation — Preprocessing & Segmentation Methods** | Sobel, Canny, Hough, Otsu, K-Means, Region Growing, Jaccard, Dice |
